# Digital Transformation in Human Resource Management & Employee Experience

## Project Overview

This project explores the role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in transforming Human Resource Management (HRM) and improving employee experience. It demonstrates the practical application of AI tools for business research, analysis, problem-solving, and customised AI assistant development.

## Task 1: Business Research Using Google NotebookLM
Link:https://notebook.google.com/notebook/ead4ce36-2f90-4e83-b5ff-d622ab986bcf

**Topic:** A Study on Digital Transformation in Human Resource Management and Its Impact on Employee Experience.

Conducted research using Google NotebookLM by collecting and analysing credible industry reports, academic publications, research articles, and government policy documents.

**Key Activities:**
- Collected and analysed relevant research sources.
- Prepared an overview of digital transformation in HRM.
- Identified major organisational challenges.
- Analysed a key business problem related to fragmented HR systems and low digital adoption.
- Conducted a SWOT analysis.
- Extracted business insights from research sources.
- Developed practical business recommendations and an implementation roadmap.

**Tool Used:** Google NotebookLM

## Task 2: Custom AI Business Assistant Using Gemini Gems
Link:https://share.gemini.google/Y8tA5bBlRoKU

**Gem Name:** HR Digital Transformation & Employee Experience Advisor.

Designed and configured a customised AI assistant using Google Gemini Gems to support HR professionals in improving digital HR operations and employee experience.

**Key Activities:**
- Defined the assistant's role, responsibilities, and target users.
- Developed detailed custom instructions and business-specific prompts.
- Configured HR-focused capabilities covering recruitment, onboarding, employee engagement, HR automation, HR analytics, and digital transformation.
- Established response formats, clarification rules, and ethical guidelines.
- Tested the assistant using 10 different real-world HR business queries.
- Evaluated its strengths, limitations, risks, and potential improvements.

**Tool Used:** Google Gemini Gems
